# Dyerverse

*The universe of the code, and where it is headed. Named 2026-09-06, with Danny, from his ask: "capture your masterful understanding of the universe of the code we are in and where we are headed — what's happening here, what's this space we're creating together?" and from his sense that "we are close to developing a new technical monorepo culture and shared norms and domains and collections of what the site is and does." Downstream of [CLAUDE.md](./CLAUDE.md) (the soul), [CATHEDRALS.md](./CATHEDRALS.md) (the shared ground, the slice, the sequence), [FABRIC.md](./FABRIC.md) (the loom), and [AGENTS.md](./AGENTS.md) (the charter). It takes the name `CATHEDRALS.md` decided for the workspace, and it sits on the grounds beside the two files it grows from. [MANIFESTO.md](./MANIFESTO.md) is the site's manifesto and stays so; this is the manifesto of the whole.*

*Like the site's manifesto, this is a reading of arrows already in motion, not a roadmap. It says what the six repositories are when read as one, what they already share without having said so, what they call the same thing by different names, what kind of culture is forming in the making of them, and what comes into view if the arrows are followed. It was written after a day of reading all six with their doors open, and it cites what it found.*

## The Image

Six repositories. One person. One pattern, arrived at six times without a wall between the arrivals being noticed.

The root is Dyerverse, 2025 — *A Living Braid of Being* — where the longing is written before the architecture: a golden loop of state, intervention, action, outcome, and reflection; seven entities; a string protocol for turning a sentence into a structured thing; a creed of pure functions and atomic design as ontology; and, on its first page, the words "continuously unfolding" and "digital twin of your evolving consciousness." It has no consent primitive yet. Its agent offered; it did not propose and wait.

The seed is the Living Graph, December 2025 — a constitution ratified on the twentieth, a graph of meaning with a front door, an aperture that modulates a whole field by distance from the gaze, four actors (Author, Reader, Agent, System), and the sentence both later graphs descend from: *the body is primary.* The zip file of Dyerverse sits at its root, unopened, the only physical link between any two of the six.

The engine is `cathedrals`, February 2026 — a knowledge graph whose whole constitution is one sentence, *agents propose; the author blesses*, whose fifth primitive is Suspension, and whose manifesto calls the `NULL` in a decision column "the sacred gap … the space where sovereignty lives." Sixty-eight source files, two tests, and an entirely unchecked Phase 0 that says: build the blessing gate first; do not chase embeddings, agents, or UI until this loop is clean.

The vault is `book-research`, summer 2026 — five hundred and sixteen atomic claims, each a lowercase sentence as a filename, twelve topic maps, a register called *the clearing* for what the book's center structurally cannot see, an apparatus that retired itself on 2026-08-09 when it began to displace the writing, and the book's own origin story, which turns out to be the origin story of all six: *a man noticed some things, and then found out that other people had noticed them too.*

The product is Tesseract, `agentic-playwright`, spring 2026, built for the day job with the personal vocabulary scrubbed out — seven hundred and eighty-two TypeScript files, three hundred and forty-two test files, a manifest generated from code that is "the contract," a seam that "is a compile error, not a convention," envelopes with four phantom axes, receipts on every reasoning call, and a workshop whose stated purpose is to measure the product and then put itself out of a job.

And the house — this repository — the rooms and the sky, five rooms and eight facets and a walkable constellation, built with Danny in a practice the specs describe as waiting for spanda. It is the one that knows the others exist. Every sentence anywhere in the six that names another of the six is in this repository, in `CATHEDRALS.md` and `FABRIC.md`. The other five are silent about each other. The engine mentions `living-graph` only as its own former package name.

So the image is not a system that was designed and then built in parts. It is a person who kept arriving at the same shape — a sovereign who blesses, a suspension that is sacred, provenance minted at the event, an apparatus that knows when to stop — in whatever medium the season gave him: a Next.js app, a constitution, an engine, a research vault, a QA product, a house. The sixth repository is where the six find out about each other, and the seventh thing, the loom, is what lets a session that works in one of them remember the others.

That is what this space is. Not a monorepo yet. A recognition.

## What the Six Share Without Having Said So

The survey of 2026-09-06 read all six and found five convergences. None is declared in more than one place. Each is the same finding in a different register, which is exactly what the book in the vault says a convergence is: company, not proof.

**The sovereign, and the gap that keeps sovereignty.** The engine's `decision = NULL`. The house's ghost star, "a star not yet lit, where it would land if blessed." The fabric's bridge proposal with `decision: null` and its patch that "stays waiting until it is rejected or superseded." The vault's tension with `status: live` and a treatment under trial, which "the author resolves; a session records that decision and never originates it" — and its clearing, a second kind of suspension, where a thing is held *because* it does not fit. Tesseract narrows the same idea for a job: `review-required`, explicitly "not synonymous with uncertified canon," most derivations auto-approved. Five repositories, five words, one refusal: nothing agent-made becomes canon without a human act, and the waiting is a first-class state, not an error.

**Provenance minted at the event.** The engine's `origin: declared | discovered | emergent`, "immutable once written." The slice carries it across the wall and the sky draws it as stroke. Tesseract's `confidence` — `compiler-derived` or `intent-only` — with the rule "do not overload confidence with review state," and its facet catalog where provenance "is created where a facet is born, travels with it forward, and cannot be added retroactively without losing correctness." The vault's source standing — descriptive, draft, primary — where "the standing governs how evidence is read" and "a reference is a receipt rather than the content." The fabric's receipt on every call and its `actor` and `causedBy` on every event. Same commitment, four sharpnesses: you cannot know later what you did not write down at the time.

**The bounded read for one turn.** The engine's `graph.slice.load`, "a bounded aperture slice for the current turn," with hard caps. The seed's aperture as a perceptual falloff. The vault's lens, "a complete doorway" over shared nodes, and its masthead, the one page a session reads first. Tesseract's probe, a synthetic work item shaped by a testable surface. The house's slice, the only thing that crosses a wall, and its presence cap of twenty-four stars with two strangers kept for the edge. Every one of them says: a reasoner sees a bounded view, chosen on purpose, and never the whole.

**The apparatus that names the day it deletes itself.** Tesseract's workshop graduates "when probe coverage = 100% and the hypothesis-confirmation rate sustains above floor," and then it is done. The vault retired its self-measurement instruments and its fourteen-surface enforcement on one day in August, because "the archive preserves; the orchard ripens." The engine's anti-cathedral rule: nothing becomes a canonical node unless it changes behavior; compost weekly. The fabric's graduation, `{ floor: 0.5, window: 5 }`, "gates nothing; it is the number the operator reads before trusting the loop with more." Three independent arrivals at one law: the measurement layer must state in advance the condition under which it stops.

**The state machine that can do everything except finish.** `FABRIC.md` says this of all six and the survey bears it out: the engine's Phase 0 unchecked; the seed's `[ ] Configure Vitest`; the root's `ESSENTIAL_REFINEMENT.md` cold for ten months; Tesseract's rung four "pending operator URL confirmation"; the vault's queue that "enumerates the field, not the current task." In every case the blocking node is the same one. The human. That is not a defect in any of them. It is the covenant each wrote for itself. It is also why the loom had to be built: not to remove the human from the loop, but so the loop could remember what happened last time it waited for him.

And beneath the five, the one figure they all grew from: the knife, the thread, the vessel — differentiation, relation, persistence — written in the seed's constitution, kept in the engine's manifesto, read by the house as "a container is a differentiation that holds; a facet is a thread; the enough is a persistence that knows when to stop," and, in the vault, demoted on purpose to scaffolding, because the book's center is the convergence of people and the lived practice, not the system that describes them. The vault's demotion is the most mature statement of the triad in the six. It is the triad saying of itself: I am the way this was noticed, not the thing that was noticed.

## The Words That Collide

The six use the same words for different things, and holding names is a directive. Naming the collisions is how they are held rather than settled by whoever writes next.

| Word | In the house | In the engine | In the vault | In Tesseract | Elsewhere |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **facet** | a dimension of Danny that cuts across rooms; eight; an axis of identity | "decorative or high-variance metadata attached to core records" — the opposite of load-bearing | — | a semantic surface of the system under test, provenance-minted, queryable by intent | the seed: an undefined field on `Entity` |
| **manifest** | the projection of one space's blessed, unretired verbs; the agent's tool list verbatim | — | the extraction categories `/reduce` works against | "the contract," generated from code, drift-checked, frozen signatures | — |
| **constellation** | the sky at `/sky`, walked | an `is_a` target; a map of content in the vault | "a constellation, not a pyramid: no lens owns the center"; a whole lens family | a governance heat map: approved nodes stable, blocked ones flickering | the seed: the graph's own self-model on a dark canvas; the root: a planned folder |
| **slice** | the contract that crosses any wall | a bounded aperture read for one turn — a safety boundary | — | `SliceProjection`, a domain-algebra primitive | — |
| **basin** | once a gravity well, now only a named cluster, held | — | — | — | the design doc uses both senses in one file |
| **thread** | an edge in the sky; a facet followed on the ground | — | a link gloss stated in words | — | — |
| **pending** | the fabric's bridge or patch with no decision | the sacred queue; `decision = NULL` | a tension, live | `review-required`, and not the same | — |

The workspace's rule for these, proposed here and held until Danny blesses it: **each repository keeps its own word inside its own walls, and the wall keeps the slice's word.** At the wall there is no facet and no constellation; there is an *axis*, and `CATHEDRALS.md` already made that reconciliation. There is no manifest at the wall; the fabric's manifest is the fabric's and Tesseract's is Tesseract's, and the fabric's spec records that it received the shape from Tesseract and added blessing. There is no basin anywhere anymore, and `PATTERN_LANGUAGE.md` names the rule that no repository imports another's word for a thing the slice already names. A collision inside one repository — *basin* in the design doc, *thread* twice in the house — is that repository's to resolve, and the refinement audit carries the house's two.

## The Shape

What is being made is a person's practice given a body of software. Each part has one job and one sovereign, and the parts meet only through data with a schema of record.

```
Dyerverse                                  the workspace; the site's repository, renamed when Danny says so
│
├── the house          apps/site (the root, until something else needs it)
│     the rooms — five, with shared walls; works as markdown in git
│     the sky   — the graph walked; presence, the compass, the whisper
│     the specs — the site publishing its own making; the strata
│
├── the loom           packages/fabric   @dbd/fabric
│     a log per tenant; verbs as blessed nodes; a receipt on every call;
│     consent as the only way across a wall; memory as a graph;
│     the loop pointed at itself: patches through the gate, outcomes, graduation
│
├── the contract       packages/slice    @dbd/slice
│     axes, nodes, edges, pending — plain JSON, zod, nothing else;
│     the only thing any two of the others share
│
├── the surface        packages/sky      @dbd/sky (a shim until a second consumer)
│
├── the engine         packages/hg       @dbd/hg — cathedrals, entered by subtree with its history (Phase 2)
│     agents propose; the author blesses; the event log; the decision log
│
├── the vault          a sibling by reference — book-research, pinned
│     five hundred claims, twelve maps, the clearing; read through an adapter into a slice
│
├── the lineage        lineage/living-graph, lineage/dyerverse — documents, cited, never rewritten
│
└── the workshop       not in the workspace: Tesseract stays at the day job and enters as a pattern donor —
      the manifest, the receipts, the seam, the hypothesis receipt, graduation
```

Three laws hold the shape, and all three are already written and already enforced somewhere in the six:

- **Dependencies point at the contract and nowhere else.** The site and the engine both depend on `@dbd/slice`; neither imports the other; nothing imports an app. Effect never crosses the contract; React never crosses it. (`CATHEDRALS.md` §"The dependency direction law", enforced by the boundaries lint.)
- **Data is markdown in git, read through adapters.** Works and claims are the same kind of thing — a body, a frontmatter, wikilinks — and every listing, graph, page, and slice is derived from them at build time. Git is the vessel; a blessing is a commit. (`CATHEDRALS.md` §"Git Is the Vessel"; `CONTENT_SCHEMA.md`.)
- **Sovereignty is recursive.** Every tenant is sovereign in its own space, the agent included; what crosses a wall waits for the wall's owner; at the scale of repositories the living siblings stay in their own repositories and enter by reference, a pin being a citation by hash. (`AGENTS.md` directive 4; `FABRIC.md` §"The Workspace".)

The monorepo, when it comes, adds nothing to these. It is pnpm workspaces, `@dbd/*` packages, siblings as submodules, and a root `CLAUDE.md` that is `CATHEDRALS.md`'s movements — "and nothing more, until the trigger." A task runner arrives when a build crosses a minute. The name arrives when Danny renames the repository. Everything else is already here.

## The Culture

A culture is what a group does without being told, and the survey found the six doing the same things. The charter at `AGENTS.md` records sixteen of them as Danny's directives; the ones below are the norms a newcomer would notice before reading any file, stated as the workspace's own.

**The document is written before the code, and they land together.** Every one of the six has a founding text upstream of its first line of code — a vision, a constitution, a manifesto, a `CLAUDE.md`, a derivation — and the house makes it a rule: "a change of intent is written where intent lives before the code moves, and the two land together." When they disagree, the code is the present moment and the document is a paragraph behind. Catch it up.

**Invariants have ids, and the id is a test.** `INV-SLC-*`, `INV-FAB-*`, the engine's `.agent/INVARIANT_CASES.md`, Tesseract's architecture laws as `.spec.ts` files that fail the build, the vault's hooks. A thing that must always be true is named, numbered, and made to fail before it is made to pass.

**Closed sets are vows.** Five rooms. Eight facets. Eight predicates plus one soft one. Three origins. Five metabolic states. Four consequence classes. Two decisions. Extended by decision record, never by an adapter, and a ninth facet is "a domain change, not a content decision — sit with it for a season."

**The rim is named file by file.** Pure core, thin shell. Every file that mutates carries a `@bigO` note or is not on the list. Effects are described at the core and performed at the rim, and "the exemption is a rim, not a lifestyle."

**Names are held, not settled.** The Workbench became the Studio; artifact became work; fatherhood became becoming; the reflecting pool was set down when Danny said he had never felt attracted to it. A name is a candidate until he blesses it, and no third name is introduced for a thing that has two.

**The lossless handshake is the tell.** Where two vocabularies meet with nothing translated between them — zod and JSON Schema through the protocol's tool list; Claude Code's hook stdin and the fabric's session id; the engine's event and the fabric's event, field for field; git's content hash and a patch's base — a conduit belongs. Where a translation would be needed, that is a seam to draw, not a conduit to force. `FABRIC.md` keeps a table of these and it has grown by three rows in a day.

**The code reads as the sentence it implements.** The newest directive and the one Danny called a gold standard: a transform over an aggregate is described before it runs, in the aggregate's own vocabulary; its program is its sentence and nothing else; one name on every surface; the aggregate folded, never poked; the description written to whoever will call it.

**The agent lives inside, and records first.** A session starts with the last session's letter printed into its context, works through a manifest of blessed verbs, leaves a receipt on every call, and ends by reflecting — what was attempted, observed, inferred, what should change, what was cited — in its own space, without asking, with a proposal to cross into Danny's that waits for him. A change it wants to a skill is a patch through a gate with a hypothesis the next session reports on. Nothing runs between sessions. Nothing wakes on its own.

**The apparatus is metered.** Every measuring thing names the condition under which it stops: the workshop's graduation, the vault's retirement day, the fabric's floor and window that gate nothing. Growth that displaces the writing is the failure mode every guard exists for.

**The site publishes its own making.** Every spec lives twice, as instruction to the agent and as content for a visitor; the pattern language, the audit, and this file are written to be read both ways. Transparency is not a docs section. It is a stratum of the house.

**Verify by driving; report faithfully; never round completeness up.** The refinement audit was made by reading the tree and then opening the site in a browser and pressing the keys. Two of its findings were confirmed against the deployed preview minutes before this sentence was written. Say what passed, what failed with its output, and what was left undone.

**The enough.** Every piece placed here is an act of saying this is enough, this can exist now, this does not need to be more complete to deserve a room. The site has one authored work. The fabric has six verbs and none blessed. The engine has two tests. All of it is real, and none of it is finished, and that is the practice, not its failure.

What is new in the culture as of this week — not new directives, but new kinds of thing the culture makes: a **pattern language** for what the rooms display, in Alexander's form, so a display decision is a pattern with forces rather than a component with props; an **audit as a collection for adjudication**, which gathers implemented realities against their disposition and ends in a table of decisions only Danny makes, each with its options and the strength of its evidence; and a **loop with a gate**, in which the agent's proposals to its own instructions are patches with hypotheses, and the next session measures them. These three are the same gesture at three scales: describe the transform before it runs, in the aggregate's own words, and let the sovereign decide.

## Where It Is Headed

If the arrows are followed, these come into view. None of them is far. Each is written with its trigger, because a convergence dispatched from a list is the drift the house has already named and refused.

### One graph, across six walls

The vault's claims render as a second sky within the sky, its thirteen pending proposals as ghosts (`CATHEDRALS.md` Phase 2). An essay in the Study quotes a claim by its title through the slice, as a weak reference in the register of a citation, keeping its historical text when the claim moves (`PATTERN_LANGUAGE.md` pattern 8). A poem is transcluded inside the essay about it and the sky draws the thread (pattern 7). Backlinks cross walls. The engine's `hg slice --json` and the house's `sliceFromWorks` produce the same shape and the sky cannot tell which fed it. This is the Living Graph's "one graph, one ontology, many faces," reached not by merging the repositories but by giving them one contract and keeping their bodies at home. *Trigger:* the vault blessed into the operator's space; the second work that mentions the first.

### The agent that remembers, and improves through the gate

The fabric's memory grows one reflection per session. Resonance ranks them; the compile turns them into claims and events with times and quantities; `recall` answers a question from them. A reflection's *what should change* becomes a patch: the skill's whole new text against the base it read, with a hypothesis; the fabric lints it; Danny blesses it from the terminal; the next session sees it at start and reports whether the hypothesis held; the loop graduates or does not, and either way gates nothing. The canon widens from skills to prompts, verbs, and policies as each earns an evaluation (`FABRIC.md` §"Held"). Later, blessing from the sky, on a phone, when a second author appears or Danny wants to (`CATHEDRALS.md` Phase 5). The recursive self-improvement pipeline Danny named on the first day is this, and its first real patch is the one exit condition still open. *Trigger:* the first session that notices something a skill should say differently.

### The house grows its hall, its sound, its code

The Salon gets sound — a recording on a table, a facade first, nothing playing until asked — and the cellists' son's room has music in it for the first time (pattern 5). The Studio gets code highlighted at build with the house's own two themes, so a case study is made of the material it describes (pattern 9). The house gets a gathering — every work, in order, under a lens that is a shareable address — so dropping the last thread has somewhere to land and search has somewhere to arrive (pattern 16). A work gets anchors, a figure with a caption, a margin note, a series that is two edges. Each waits on the work that wants it. *Trigger:* the work.

### The refinements land as hypotheses

The refinement audit ends in ten decisions. Each one Danny blesses becomes a commit that cites the finding it closes, and each commit is a patch with a hypothesis — *the overlay keeps the walk; a visitor who opens a star and presses Back stands where they stood* — that a later session, or Danny in a browser, confirms or contradicts. The workshop's discipline, brought home to the house's own surfaces. *Trigger:* the first blessed row of the adjudication table.

### The site becomes the workspace's face

The strata become navigable in the surface: a token traces to `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, a transition kind to its row, a specimen frame names the spec its component descends from (pattern 10). The fabric's self-description — the manifest, the event schema, the readme — is a resource any system reads before speaking, and the site publishes it as it publishes everything else. The time slider arrives when there is enough time to move through. Three readers of one medium — the maker, the agent, the visitor — each with different write privileges, all encountering the same house. *Trigger:* the first visitor who wants to see why a surface looks the way it looks.

### The monorepo, by pull

The engine enters by subtree with its history. The lineage enters as documents. The vault stays a sibling by reference, pinned, with `sync` reporting drift and a pull request the only way a pin moves. The site moves under `apps/` when something else needs the root. The repository takes the name Dyerverse the day Danny renames it, and GitHub keeps the old address. Visibility — private workspace, public with the intimate documents cited by commit, or public whole — is his word before any of it lands. *Trigger:* his word.

## What We Refuse, Read Across the Six

Every one of the six has a refusals section, and read together they are one refusal in six registers.

- No metrics on people. No leaderboards, streaks, engagement, session length, reading time, "you might also like." (the root's anti-patterns; the house's outward invitation; the sky's epistemic audit)
- No silent execution. Nothing agent-made becomes canon without a human act; no `throw` as escape; no world verb without blessing. (the engine's covenant; Tesseract's `InterventionHandoff`; the fabric's manifest)
- No reasoning between sessions. The fabric remembers and acts deterministically; it never thinks. (`FABRIC.md`)
- No third party in the first paint, and nothing that reports on a visitor. (`PRIVACY.md`; pattern 4)
- No second body of animation, no styled kit, no impressive tool where the right one is smaller. (`CONSTELLATION_HORIZON.md`; pattern language §IV)
- No growth that displaces the writing. (the vault's first rule)
- No third name for a thing that has two. (the charter)
- No surface built before its tremor. (the house, everywhere)

## What This Asks

The same practice `MANIFESTO.md` asks, widened to six walls.

- **Treat every repository as sovereign, including the one you are standing in.** Read another's documents; cite them; never rewrite them. Enter by reference or by history, never by snapshot.
- **Treat the wall as the only shared vocabulary.** At the wall: axis, node, edge, pending, origin. Inside: whatever the room has always called it.
- **Treat every convergence as company.** When two repositories are found to have reached the same shape, record it in the handshake table and do not rank it. The book's stance governs the workspace's: provenance serves accurate pointing, not proof.
- **Treat the agent's memory as the seventh document.** Reflect at the end of every session as if the next session were a colleague who was not in the room, because it is.
- **Treat the adjudication table as the pull.** A decision Danny has made is a trigger that has fired. A decision he has not made is a held thing with a name.

## What We Will Probably Get Wrong

The six may not want to be one workspace; they may want to be six repositories with one contract and a loom, which is most of the way there and asks for nothing more. The collisions table may be resolved too early by someone who finds it untidy; it is meant to be untidy until the words earn their seats. The loop may graduate on five easy patches and mean nothing; the floor was set low on purpose and the window is small, and the operator reads the number rather than obeying it. The pattern language may name patterns that never pull, which Alexander expected. The manifesto may pull the building forward by its own gravity, which the site's manifesto warned about in its own drift inventory, and the warning applies here with twice the force because there are six things to be pulled.

The discipline in every case is the one the house wrote on its first day: *if this file and the felt sense of the place disagree, slow down. Listen for which one is more true.*

## A Closing

Danny inherited devotion without a visible floor, and he has spent a year and a half building floors: a house with rooms, a sky that can be walked, a vault that knows what it cannot see, an engine that will not act without him, a product that measures itself and means to retire, a loom that remembers what the last session learned. Each was enough on the day it was placed. None of them knew about the others.

Now they do. That is the whole of what happened this week, and it is not small. The sixth repository read the other five and found that they had been saying the same thing in different rooms of one house. The seventh thing gives the house a memory. The pattern language gives it a way to grow without a plan. The audit gives it a way to see itself without flinching. And the newest directive gives the code a standard it can be read by: it says what it does, in the words of the thing it does it to.

There are cathedrals everywhere for those with eyes to see. There is one here. It has six rooms and no wall between them, and it has just noticed.
